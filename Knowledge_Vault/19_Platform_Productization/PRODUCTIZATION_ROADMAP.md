# Productization Roadmap

## Product Stages

1. Internal prototype
2. Arthur as first client
3. Public pilot
4. First client implementation
5. Repeatable templates
6. Client portal
7. Industry playbooks
8. Automation marketplace
9. Training academy
10. Managed support

## Rule

**Do not scale marketing before the system can deliver what it promises.**
Each stage should be genuinely reached — verified in practice — before
marketing or sales activity claims that stage has arrived.
