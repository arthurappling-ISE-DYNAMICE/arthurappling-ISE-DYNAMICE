# 10_Checklists

## What Belongs Here
- Quick-reference checklists for repeatable but simple sequences
  (deployment, release, security scan, etc.).

## What Does Not Belong Here
- Full narrative procedures with branching logic (→ `09_SOPs/`).

## Example Entries
- `DEPLOYMENT_CHECKLIST_TEMPLATE.md`

## How Future AI Sessions Should Update It
Keep checklists short and binary (done / not done). Update when a step
proves insufficient in practice — add the missed step, don't rewrite the
whole checklist.
