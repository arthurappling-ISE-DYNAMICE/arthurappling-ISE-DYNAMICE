# Client Delivery Framework

Prime Pathwy should not sell "AI." Prime Pathwy should sell outcomes.

## Client Delivery Phases

1. **Discovery** — understand the business, industry, and stated goals.
2. **Diagnosis** — identify the real pain points and bottlenecks, not just
   the symptoms the client leads with.
3. **Workflow mapping** — document current process, tools, and staff/
   customer workflow.
4. **System architecture** — design the SYS_OS-based solution that fits
   the diagnosed need.
5. **Prototype** — build a fast, honest proof-of-concept on the owned
   platform.
6. **Dashboard build** — build the actual client-facing dashboard modules.
7. **Backend/security setup** — auth, persistence, RLS isolation, and any
   integrations, verified before anything ships.
8. **Client training** — hand off with real training, not just a login.
9. **Deployment** — go live using a validated, curated deploy process.
10. **Support/optimization** — ongoing support and iteration after launch.

## Standards That Apply at Every Phase

- **Client trust standard:** clear diagnosis, honest scope, secure data
  handling, repeatable workflows, training and handoff, supportability,
  measurable business improvement.
- **No smash-and-grab service model.** This is a relationship built for
  repeat trust, not a one-off transaction.
- **Quality-first implementation.** Security and correctness come before
  speed or visual polish.
- **Measurable business improvement.** Every engagement should be able to
  point to a concrete before/after.
- **Payment plans / tiered delivery** are acceptable, but scope must stay
  controlled — see `18_Monetization_and_Pricing/PRICING_STRATEGY_NOTES.md`.
