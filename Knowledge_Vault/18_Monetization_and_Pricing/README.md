# 18_Monetization_and_Pricing

## Purpose
Preserve Prime Pathwy's pricing logic and value positioning so pricing
decisions stay consistent and defensible across engagements.

## What Belongs Here
- Pricing strategy notes and tier structure.
- Reusable value-proposition language.

## What Does Not Belong Here
- Actual client quotes, invoices, or contract terms — those are business
  records, not vault documentation.
- Marketing content ideas unrelated to pricing (→ `08_Marketing/`).

## How Future AI Sessions Should Update It
- Update pricing notes when Arthur makes an explicit pricing decision —
  don't invent tiers or numbers speculatively.
- Keep value-proposition language reusable and generic; client-specific
  pitches belong in `07_Client_Playbooks/`, not here.

## Warning
**Never store secrets.** No client payment details, account numbers, or
financial credentials in any file here.
