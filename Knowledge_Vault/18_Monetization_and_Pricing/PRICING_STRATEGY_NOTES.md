# Pricing Strategy Notes

Pricing should reflect:

- Diagnosis
- Build complexity
- Backend/security requirements
- Integrations
- Training
- Support
- Ongoing updates

## Possible Tiers

- **Assessment** — discovery and diagnosis engagement.
- **Starter setup** — basic system setup for a small operation.
- **Dashboard build** — a client-facing SYS_OS dashboard module.
- **Full SYS_OS implementation** — complete backend, security, and
  dashboard build.
- **Monthly support/optimization** — ongoing maintenance and iteration.
- **Premium custom automation** — bespoke automation built on top of the
  platform.

## Rules

- Payment plans are acceptable if scope is controlled.
- Do not underprice work that requires security, backend, and maintenance
  — those costs are real and recurring, and pricing must reflect that.
