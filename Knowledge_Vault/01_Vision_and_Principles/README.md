# 01_Vision_and_Principles

## What Belongs Here
- Core business principles that guide every decision.
- The "why" behind Prime Pathwy that doesn't change often.
- Statements of what the company is and is not.

## What Does Not Belong Here
- Specific technical architecture (→ `02_Architecture/`).
- Specific engineering decisions (→ `03_Engineering_Decisions/`).
- Anything that changes month to month — this folder is for durable
  principles, not current status.

## Example Entries
- `PRIME_PATHWY_CORE_PRINCIPLES.md`

## How Future AI Sessions Should Update It
Update only when Arthur explicitly redefines a core principle. Do not add
speculative or aspirational principles without his sign-off — this file
should reflect standards Prime Pathwy actually holds itself to.
