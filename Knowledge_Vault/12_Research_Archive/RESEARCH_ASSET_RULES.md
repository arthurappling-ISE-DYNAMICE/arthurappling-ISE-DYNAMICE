# Research Asset Rules

- Research assets are not automatically roadmaps — finding something
  interesting does not mean it becomes a mission.
- Extract useful patterns only. Do not copy leaked prompts, proprietary
  code, or competitor implementations directly.
- Do not let outside tools, trends, or research change the current mission
  (see root `CLAUDE.md` → Current Mission Discipline).
- Classify every research asset as one of:
  - **KEEP** — proven and durable, move to the appropriate numbered folder.
  - **PARK** — plausible future value → `06_Future_Features_Shelf/`.
  - **DISCARD** — no lasting value, do not keep the file.
  - **IMPLEMENT_LATER** — validated but deferred, note revisit condition.
