# 12_Research_Archive

## What Belongs Here
- Raw research assets, articles, external prompts, or reference material
  that may contain useful patterns but hasn't been classified yet.

## What Does Not Belong Here
- Anything already classified into Competitor Intelligence, Future
  Features, or Engineering Decisions — move it out once classified.

## Example Entries
- `RESEARCH_ASSET_RULES.md`

## How Future AI Sessions Should Update It
Classify every asset as KEEP, PARK, DISCARD, or IMPLEMENT_LATER (see
`RESEARCH_ASSET_RULES.md`). Don't let raw research silently become roadmap.
