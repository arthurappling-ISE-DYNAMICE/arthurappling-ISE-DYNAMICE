# Competitor Analysis — WebAppDev-style Rapid Build Model

- **Their model:** voice idea → AI clarification → full-stack build → live
  subdomain, delivered fast.
- **Strength:** fast visual prototype — the client sees something real
  almost immediately, which builds momentum and confidence.
- **Business model:** free first build → monthly retainer for ongoing
  hosting/support/iteration.
- **Useful pattern to extract:** AI-guided client interview — using
  structured AI-driven questions to pull requirements out of a client who
  can't articulate a spec themselves.
- **Useful pattern to extract:** rapid proof-of-concept — showing a working
  artifact early to build trust and buy-in before deep engineering.
- **Caution — lock-in risk:** the client's product lives on the vendor's
  platform; if the relationship ends, the client may lose their build.
- **Caution — generic MVP architecture risk:** speed-first builds often
  reuse a generic template, so the delivered product may not fit long-term
  security/scale needs.
- **Prime Pathwy adaptation:** borrow the AI-guided interview and rapid
  prototype patterns, but keep the client on an owned, verified, secure
  backend (SYS_OS) rather than a disposable generic scaffold — speed to
  demo without sacrificing the trust standard.
