# 05_Competitor_Intelligence

## What Belongs Here
- Analysis of competitor products, tactics, and business models.
- Each entry should include: their model, their strengths, their business
  model, useful patterns to extract, cautions/risks, and the Prime Pathwy
  adaptation.

## What Does Not Belong Here
- Copied prompts, copied code, or copied UI — extract the pattern, never the
  implementation.
- Unverified rumors about competitors without noting them as such.

## Example Entries
- `WEBAPPDEV_ANALYSIS.md`
- `LINDY_ANALYSIS.md`

## How Future AI Sessions Should Update It
Always end an entry with an explicit "Prime Pathwy adaptation" section so the
intelligence turns into an actionable, differentiated take rather than a
copy target.
